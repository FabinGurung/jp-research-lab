Read the analysis assumptions

Linear elastic, unfactored and symmetric gravity loads; fixed column bases and rigid roof joints. No seismic, wind, foundation, RC capacity or rebar design. The slab is a load-distribution panel, not a finite-element shell.

Clockwise member end actions are positive in the calculation trace. Beam end hogging is displayed as a positive magnitude in this table.
