The proposal

An elegant yet practical Damauli wedding plan built around guest comfort, cultural traditions, reliable catering and transparent supplier comparisons. The five-venue shortlist is for site inspection and quotation; no reservation, rate or event permission is implied.

01 · A celebration rooted in Damauli

JP Enterprise is the coordinating organisation for the proposed ceremony and reception in Damauli, Byas Municipality, Tanahun. Vendors may be contracted from Damauli, nearby Bandipur/Shuklagandaki or Pokhara where logistics and cost are suitable.

Before selection, confirm the auspicious date and ritual needs, seated and buffet guest count, accommodation, monsoon/rain shelter, parking, accessible routes, kitchen facilities, electrical backup, wedding use permissions, cancellation clauses and written catering inclusions.

02 · Five potential wedding venues

01 · Hotel + banquet

Siddhartha Hotel Damauli

Multiple event-hall formats, guest rooms and integrated food service. Operator material describes three halls, 34 rooms and banqueting capacity up to about 300, depending on setup.

Best for: wedding reception with guest accommodation and bundled catering.

Confirm wedding seating, hall allocation and in-house menus.

02 · Local banquet

Nimto Party Palace

Dedicated Damauli/Byas party-palace option. Evaluate venue layout, dining and parking provisions during a physical survey.

Best for: conventional local reception and straightforward event management.

Confirm usable capacity, covered space and kitchen service.

03 · Celebration hall

Royal Palace Damauli

Event venue in the Malebagar/Vyas Marga area associated with celebrations and family functions.

Best for: wedding stage, family guests and reception programme.

Obtain layout, power, toilets, parking and full quotation.

04 · Hotel + catering

Santosh Hotel

Damauli hospitality provider with public references to party and wedding catering and hall/conference facilities.

Best for: a simplified catering-led ceremony or smaller reception.

Wedding suitability and capacity require direct confirmation.

05 · Civic venue

Damauli Sabha Hall

A local assembly/community facility near the BP Banbatika area, potentially suitable for an organised event only if municipal rules allow private wedding functions.

Best for: a separately catered programme if officially permitted.

Written municipal event-use permission is essential.

Recommended first inspection: Siddhartha Hotel Damauli for its combined hall, catering and lodging model. Inspect Nimto and Royal Palace in parallel for comparable banquet offers. This is not an availability confirmation.

04 · Ceremony and guest experience

Suggested design language: ivory, maroon and warm gold, using delicate floral motifs, traditional textiles, tasteful mandap decor and generous daylight. Keep the ritual area distinct from buffet flow and vehicle access. Stage a welcome desk, seating for elders, family photography zone, first-aid point and weather-protected catering circulation.

A wedding may include Mehendi, Haldi, main ceremony and reception; each requires a separate guest count, menu, ceremonial timing and responsible contact.

06 · Evidence-based venue choice

Score venues from 1 to 5 after the site visit and a comparable quotation. The weights are: seating capacity 25%, final cost 20%, access 15%, catering quality 15%, accommodation 10%, weather/power resilience 10%, and ceremony aesthetic fit 5%.

Sᵢ = Σ (wₖ × sᵢₖ)    where Σ wₖ = 1

Reject any venue that fails required permission, emergency-access or safe-capacity checks even if its numerical score is high.

07 · JP Enterprise operating procedures

  1. Client brief: document date, guest bands, ritual requirements, access needs and spending limit.
  2. Site survey: inspect the hall, washrooms, kitchen, exits, parking and power backup.
  3. One RFQ for all: obtain itemised supplier scope, fees, inclusions and dated validity.
  4. Menu tasting: confirm portions, vegetarian separation, allergy controls and staff plan.
  5. Written contracts: approve only clearly itemised deposits, cancellation and overtime terms.
  6. Service integration: allocate one named coordinator and a vendor arrival schedule.
  7. Event-day control: manage guest flow, vendor check-in, issue record and emergency contacts.
  8. Closeout: confirm final counts, bills, equipment return, cleaning and feedback.

09 · Safety and contingencies

Prepare wet-weather cover; verify local roadway conditions; test backup electricity; protect temporary cabling; allocate clean water and wash facilities; route elderly guests without stairs where possible; create safe fire exits and first-aid contact plans. Supplier arrangements need written change and cancellation terms.
