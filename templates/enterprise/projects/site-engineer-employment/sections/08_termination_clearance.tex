\docsection{Separation, Notice and Clearance}
Termination and notice shall follow applicable Nepalese law, including any more
favourable lawful term. Service-based statutory notice, lawful payment in lieu,
and applicable exceptions shall be respected.

On separation the employee shall hand over company property, project records,
incomplete work and assigned responsibilities. The employer shall settle lawful
wages, leave encashment and benefits within the prescribed period. An experience
certificate giving the service period and designation shall be issued on request
where required by law. Clearance shall not be used to withhold statutory dues.
