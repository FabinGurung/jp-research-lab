\docsection{Separation, Notice and Clearance}
Either party may end employment only by procedures allowed under Nepalese law.
Unless a lawful exception or more favourable enforceable term applies, prior
notice is one day for service of up to four weeks, seven days for service
from four weeks to one year, and thirty days after one year of service.
Lawful payment in lieu of notice may apply.

On separation the employee shall hand over company property, project records,
incomplete work and assigned responsibilities. The employer shall settle lawful
wages, leave encashment and benefits within the prescribed period. An experience
certificate stating the period of service and designation shall be issued on
request where required by law. Clearance shall not be used to withhold
statutory dues.
