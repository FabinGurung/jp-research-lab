\docsection{Work Schedule and Site Operations}
Normal duty is 08:00--17:00 on rostered workdays, including one hour for lunch,
ordinarily 12:00--13:00. Ordinary work is eight hours per day and must comply with
applicable weekly-hour limits and weekly rest requirements.

Critical concrete pours, structural activities and time-sensitive inspections may
require scheduled reporting before 06:00 or an authorized night assignment continuing
past 23:59. These shall be lawful shifts or approved overtime, with recorded hours,
required breaks, safe working arrangements and statutory compensation.
Such assignments do not waive working-time or overtime limits.
