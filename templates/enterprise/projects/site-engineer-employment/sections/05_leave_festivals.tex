\docsection{Weekly Rest, Leave and Festivals}
The employee shall receive weekly rest, statutory public and festival holidays,
paid home leave, sick leave and other entitlements under applicable law.
The employer shall announce the annual leave and holiday schedule and consider
applicable Government of Nepal and Nepal Rastra Bank holiday calendars.
A bank closure does not automatically close every construction site.
Lawful alternative rest or compensation applies where essential holiday work is required.
The annual festival expense and other mandatory payments shall be provided separately
from the stated monthly remuneration where required by law.
