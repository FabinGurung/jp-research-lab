\docsection{Medical, Insurance and Social Security}
The employer shall provide occupational health and safety arrangements, required
protective equipment and workplace injury reporting. Where the Labour Act applies,
medical insurance of at least NPR 1,00,000 per year and accident insurance
coverage of at least NPR 7,00,000, or any higher coverage subsequently
required by law, shall be provided with the prescribed premium arrangements.
Eligible Social Security Fund coverage may satisfy corresponding statutory
requirements to the extent permitted by law. Employer-paid obligations shall
not be improperly deducted from the stated monthly remuneration.
