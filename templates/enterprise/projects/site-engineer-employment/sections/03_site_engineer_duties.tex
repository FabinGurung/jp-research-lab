\docsection{Duties and Responsibilities}
The Site Engineer shall work within assigned professional authority and approved designs, applicable standards and the employer's lawful instructions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Review approved architectural and structural drawings, specifications, revisions, site levels and setting-out data.
\item Supervise excavation, reinforcement, formwork, concreting, masonry, finishes and service interfaces; confirm measurements and workmanship.
\item Inspect site health, safety and environmental conditions and escalate hazards, incidents and nonconforming work.
\item Maintain daily site diaries, progress reports, measurements, workforce and equipment records, photographs and instructions.
\item Read and acknowledge designated project notifications and communications in email, Slack, Discord and employer-approved channels.
\item Maintain attendance, expenses, delivery and resource registers in approved Excel workbooks, Google Sheets and company forms.
\item Report progress, actual starts and finishes, remaining work, dependencies and schedule constraints in Primavera P6 or employer-approved programmes.
\item Arrange material availability, inspections, tests, procurement requests and construction interfaces with supervisors and contractors.
\item Maintain inspection checklists, clarification requests, site instructions, progress photographs and quality records.
\item Assist with joint measurements, quantities, bills, variations, payment checks and project handover documentation.
\item Escalate changes affecting safety, design or contractual scope for proper authorization; do not independently change approved structural designs.
\item Attend site meetings, prepare weekly progress updates and coordinate with managers, consultants, suppliers and site teams.
\end{enumerate}
