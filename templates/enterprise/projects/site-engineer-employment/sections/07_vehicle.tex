\docsection{Employee-Owned Vehicle Servicing}
When the employee uses an owned roadworthy vehicle for approved work travel,
the employer shall reimburse actual routine servicing costs, supported by receipts,
up to NPR \VehicleCap\ in each consecutive \VehicleInterval-month period of service.
Higher invoices require advance written approval. Unused quarterly limits do not
accumulate. This benefit is separate from monthly fuel/site-travel allowances.
