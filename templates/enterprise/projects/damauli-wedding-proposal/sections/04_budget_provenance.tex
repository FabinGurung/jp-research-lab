\section{Planning formulas}
Let $G$ denote guests, $P$ food cost per guest, $V$ venue rental, $D$ decoration, $A$ sound/power, $F$ photography, $T$ transport/lodging, $S$ other services, $r$ contingency fraction, and $\tau$ quotation-specific tax/surcharge fraction.

\begin{align}
B &= GP + V + D + A + F + T + S,\\
C &= rB,\\
E &= (B+C)(1+\tau),\\
\Delta E &= \Delta G\,P(1+r)(1+\tau).
\end{align}

The last expression assumes fixed venue and staffing costs, unchanged per-guest meal pricing and unchanged tax treatment. If supplier prices are tax-inclusive, do not apply tax again. Example only: 200 people at NPR 1,500 per person gives NPR 300,000 catering cost before other charges. It is not an observed supplier quotation.

\section{Venue evaluation}
Score verified evidence on a five-point scale with weights: capacity (25\%), total cost (20\%), access (15\%), catering quality (15\%), lodging (10\%), resilience (10\%), atmosphere (5\%).
\begin{equation}
S_i=\sum_{k=1}^{7} w_k\,s_{ik},
\qquad \sum_{k=1}^{7}w_k=1.
\end{equation}
Permissions, capacity and life safety are mandatory rather than averaged away by the score.
