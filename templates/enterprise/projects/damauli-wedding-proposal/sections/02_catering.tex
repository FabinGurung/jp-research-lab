\subsection{Sample food-service structure}
Welcome drink; seasonal vegetarian snacks and an optional meat choice; dal, rice, vegetables, achar, salad and paneer/meat alternatives; dessert; tea and coffee. Separate serving plans may be needed for Mehendi, Haldi, main ceremony and reception.

\subsection{Food-safety and service checks}
Require clean water, allergy information, hygienic holding temperatures, adequate servers, vegetarian separation, washing stations, safe gas/power use, waste removal and final quantities. A sample menu does not confirm a vendor's offering.
