\section{Wedding atmosphere and guest experience}
Recommended visual palette: ivory, maroon and warm gold. Organise a welcoming entrance, floral ceremony focal point, respectful ritual sight-lines, elderly seating, family-photography corner and a separate buffet area without obstructing exits.

\section{JP Enterprise operating procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Document the family brief, auspicious time, attendance bands and spending ceiling.
\item Inspect the venue, permitted usage, emergency routes and weather protection.
\item Invite comparable itemised bids with identical scopes and dates.
\item Carry out menu tasting, food hygiene and allergen checks.
\item Obtain written contracts, cancellation schedules and invoices before deposits.
\item Assign one event coordinator and a named lead for each vendor.
\item Keep an on-site check-in, variation and incident log.
\item Reconcile delivered headcount, equipment, invoices and clean-up after the event.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Essential contingencies}
Weatherproof ceremony space; backed-up electricity; clean food-handling and water access; protected cables and fire exits; elder-friendly transport; parking marshals; vendor escalation and first-aid arrangement.
