\section{Executive proposal}
JP Enterprise proposes an organised wedding ceremony and reception for Person A and Person B in Damauli, Tanahun. Nearby Pokhara, Bandipur and Shuklagandaki provide optional suppliers. Dates, prices, bookings, permissions and room allocations require written confirmation from the venue and family.

\section{Five Damauli venue options}
\subsection{Siddhartha Hotel Damauli}
An integrated hotel and banquet option with event halls, food service and guest rooms. The operator describes three halls, 34 rooms and approximate large-event capacity up to 300 under its stated arrangements. Confirm actual wedding seating layout and in-house catering.

\subsection{Nimto Party Palace}
A local banquet option for conventional family wedding celebrations. Survey covered dining space, capacity, kitchen logistics and parking.

\subsection{Royal Palace Damauli}
An event venue in the Malebagar / Vyas Marga locality. Verify stage, sound, toilets, electrical capacity and accommodation strategy.

\subsection{Santosh Hotel}
A Damauli hotel advertising wedding / party catering and event-related facilities. Obtain precise suitable-hall and guest-limit confirmation.

\subsection{Damauli Sabha Hall}
A civic assembly facility in the Byas municipality area. Use for a private marriage event only if formally permitted by the relevant municipal venue authority.

\section{Common due-diligence checklist}
Compare each offer on the same guest number, actual seated capacity, venue hire, taxes, catering, bathrooms, rain shelter, parking, generator, ceremonial times, music rules, accessibility, first-aid arrangements and cancellation terms.
